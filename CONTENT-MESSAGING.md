## 3. About Page Copy

**Page Title:** `Our Story — PrintPlayHouse`  
**Meta description:** `PrintPlayHouse was born from pure love — for design, for play, for the way clothing can make you feel. Read our story.`

---

**Body:**

### It started with a feeling.

Not a business plan. Not a market gap. A feeling.

The feeling of putting on something that just *fits* — not just on your body, but in your life. Something you reach for again and again. Something that says something without you having to say a word.

That feeling is what we chase at PrintPlayHouse. We call it Pure Love.

### What pure love means to us.

It means we care about the details other brands skip. The weight of the fleece. The feel of the print on your fingers. The way the fit moves when you do. We obsess over these things so you don't have to think about them — you just feel them.

Pure Love also means the people behind PrintPlayHouse. The designers who sketch at midnight. The makers who take care with every cut. And you — the person who wears what we make. You're part of this too.

### PrintPlayHouse is for you.

You don't have to be anything to wear our clothes. You don't have to belong to a scene or follow a trend. You just have to be yourself.

That's enough. That's everything.

**Welcome to the house.**
