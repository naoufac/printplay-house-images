## 3. Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Warm** — speak like a friend who genuinely cares
- **Playful** — don't take yourself too seriously; surprise people
- **Honest** — no fake hype; let the product speak
- **Encouraging** — lift people up, celebrate their choices

### Writing Principles
- Use "you" and "your" — make it personal
- Short sentences. Clear ideas. Real feelings.
- Lead with emotion, follow with detail
- Avoid jargon, buzzwords, and hollow superlatives
- "Pure Love" as a recurring anchor — use it meaningfully, not mechanically

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Inspiring, emotional, inviting |
| Product descriptions | Warm, descriptive, sensory |
| Email subject lines | Playful, punchy, curious |
| Social captions | Casual, relatable, emoji-friendly |
| Error pages / support | Empathetic, helpful, light |

## 4. Visual Identity

### 4.1 Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Love Red | `#E8294C` | CTAs, accents, logo mark |
| Secondary | Warm Cream | `#FDF6EC` | Backgrounds, cards |
| Accent 1 | Playful Yellow | `#FFD166` | Highlights, badges, tags |
| Accent 2 | Soft Blush | `#F4A7B9` | Secondary backgrounds, hover states |
| Dark | Ink Black | `#1A1A1A` | Body text, primary type |
| Mid | Warm Gray | `#6B6B6B` | Supporting text, borders |
| Light | Cloud White | `#FFFFFF` | Page backgrounds, product backdrops |

### 4.2 Typography

| Role | Typeface | Style | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Hero | **Playfair Display** | Bold Italic | Hero headlines, campaign titles |
| Heading | **DM Sans** | Bold (700) | Section headings, product names |
| Body | **DM Sans** | Regular (400) | Descriptions, paragraphs |
| Label / Tag | **DM Mono** | Regular | SKUs, size labels, metadata |

> **Fallback stack:** `"DM Sans", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`

### 4.3 Logo Guidelines

- The logo features a stylized **house** outline with a **heart** integrated into the roofline
- The wordmark reads **PrintPlayHouse** — set in DM Sans Bold, all lowercase preferred
- Minimum size: 120px wide in digital contexts
- Clear space: equal to the height of the "h" in the wordmark on all sides

**Logo variations:**
- Full lockup (icon + wordmark) — default
- Icon only — social avatars, favicon, embroidery
- Wordmark only — horizontal banners, email headers

**Never:**
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Place on busy backgrounds without a protective container
- Use brand colors on brand colors (e.g. Love Red on Soft Blush)

## 5. Photography & Imagery

### Style Pillars
1. **Authentic** — real people in real settings, not overly staged
2. **Joyful** — movement, laughter, genuine expression
3. **Print-forward** — the print is always the hero; showcase it clearly
4. **Inclusive** — diverse models, body types, ages, backgrounds

### Shot Types (per product — see `IMAGE-CONVENTIONS.md`)
- **Product front** — clean white/cream background, centered, full garment visible
- **Detail closeup** — texture, print quality, hardware, stitching
- **Lifestyle** — model in context (street, home, outdoors), shows fit and feel
- **UGC / Social** — lo-fi, candid energy; styled for Instagram/TikTok

### Photography Do's & Don'ts
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Natural light or soft studio light | Harsh flash, unnatural shadows |
| Show the full print clearly | Fold or bunch fabric over the print |
| Smile, movement, genuine emotion | Blank expressions, stiff poses |
| Consistent cream/white backdrops for product shots | Busy or distracting backgrounds |

## 7. Brand Story (Long Form)

We call it Pure Love. And we mean it.
